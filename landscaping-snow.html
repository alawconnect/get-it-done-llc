Landscaping & snow

From the beds along your front walk to the driveway after a winter storm, plan the outdoor work your property needs each season.

Scope of work

Landscaping
A single planting bed or a whole yard can be the starting point. Identify the areas you want changed and the plants or features you want to keep.
Leaf cleanup
Leaves collect differently on lawns, in beds and along fence lines. Identify the areas to clear and any gates or narrow passages that affect access.
Snow plowing
For driveway and parking areas, the layout matters as much as the size. Note entrances to keep clear and where plowed snow can go.

Planning your project

A wide photo of the area and rough dimensions help describe an outdoor job. Include the season or date you have in mind and any access constraints.
